1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your preferences, measure traffic, and (where you consent) show relevant advertising.

When you first visit the site you can accept or decline non-essential cookies via our cookie banner. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.
